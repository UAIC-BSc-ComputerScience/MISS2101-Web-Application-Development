Acest director contine o serie de exemple referitoare la validarea datelor RDF
prin SHACL -- a se consulta si prelegerea de la curs:
<https://profs.info.uaic.ro/sabin.buraga/teach/courses/wade/web-film.html#week6>.

* mobile-application-shacl.ttl - restrictii SHACL exprimate in RDF (formatul Turtle)
                                 ce vizeaza constrangerile suplimentare asupra
                                 instantelor din clasa (conceptul) MobileApplication 
                                 specificat(a) la
                                 <https://schema.org/MobileApplication>
* mobile-application-angryprofs.ttl - date RDF de validat (aici, fapte despre jocul
                                 fictiv Angry Profs); datele nu sunt valide conform
                                 regulilor SHACL de mai sus
* mobile-application-validation-report.ttl - raportul RDF (in Turtle) emis de 
                                 un instrument Web de validare precum: 
                                 <https://shacl.org/playground/>,  
                                 <https://shacl-playground.zazuko.com/>,
                                 <https://shacl-play.sparna.fr/play/>.
